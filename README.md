# Bybit How About It? Bybit Introduction and Review

Bybit stands as one of the world's largest cryptocurrency exchanges, offering comprehensive trading solutions for both beginners and professional traders. Founded in 2018 by Ben Zhou and headquartered in Dubai, UAE, the platform serves over 40 million global users with advanced derivatives trading, spot trading, and various crypto financial services. ## Bybit Reviews

Bybit maintains mixed user reviews across different platforms, with ratings varying significantly based on user experiences. ### Key Trading Features

Bybit's VIP program offers significant benefits for high-volume traders, including reduced trading fees, higher withdrawal limits, dedicated customer support, and exclusive event access. The platform supports various trading products including spot trading with over 400 cryptocurrency pairs, derivatives trading with up to 100x leverage, and innovative features like pre-market trading. Security measures include multi-signature wallets, two-factor authentication, and cold storage for user funds, though the February 2025 security incident highlighted vulnerabilities in third-party integrations.

### Fee Structure and Costs

Trading fees on Bybit remain competitive within the industry, with derivatives trading starting at 0.02% for makers and 0.055% for takers. Spot trading fees are standardized at 0.1% for both makers and takers, with reductions available through the VIP program based on trading volume and asset holdings. Withdrawal fees vary by cryptocurrency, with Bitcoin withdrawals costing 0.0005 BTC and Ethereum withdrawals at 0.005 ETH as of 2025. The platform also offers fiat-to-crypto trading with fees starting at 0.15% for makers and 0.2% for takers.
